# Security and operations escalation paths

## Standard escalation trigger

Escalate to the incident commander and/or service owner when the impact crosses defined customer or business thresholds, or when an operational workaround is no longer safe.

## Security escalation trigger

Escalate immediately when the event includes suspicious or confirmed malicious activity, evidence of data exposure, or unauthorized access patterns.

## Recommended routing

- Incident response: service owner + IC + technical lead
- Security: SIRT + SecOps + IC
- Executive or customer impact: communications lead + leadership escalation path

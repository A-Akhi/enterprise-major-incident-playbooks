# Contributing

Thank you for contributing to the Enterprise Major Incident Management playbook set.

## Scope

Contributions should improve operational clarity, response consistency, and service resiliency. Good submissions include:

- clearer incident command playbooks
- stronger escalation procedures
- more actionable communication templates
- problem management and post-incident follow-up improvements
- reliability or governance references that reduce operational churn

## Standards

- Prefer Markdown with clean headings, short sections, and scan-friendly checklists.
- Keep examples generic and free of customer, personnel, or sensitive operational data.
- Document prerequisites, owners, triggers, and follow-up actions clearly.
- Reference local policy and approval paths explicitly where appropriate.
- Use relative links and avoid hidden repository assumptions.

## Review checklist

Before opening a pull request, confirm:

- the document fits the target section (`playbooks/`, `governance/`, `templates/`, etc.)
- terminology is consistent with incident, problem, and reliability operations language
- links resolve correctly from the repository root
- the change improves clarity during an active major incident, not just documentation aesthetics

By contributing, you agree that your submissions are provided under the repository's MIT license.

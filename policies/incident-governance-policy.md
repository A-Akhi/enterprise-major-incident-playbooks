# Incident governance policy

## Policy statement

All high-severity incidents must have a single accountable Incident Commander, a documented decision log, and a defined communication cadence. Teams must preserve evidence when required, avoid unsupported root-cause assertions, and ensure follow-up actions are assigned for closure.

## Required controls

- clear incident ownership and role assignment
- documented timeline and action tracking
- risk-based communication and escalation
- evidence preservation for suspected security or forensic investigations
- post-incident review and corrective action tracking

## Enforcement

Operational and engineering leaders are responsible for ensuring local implementation matches the policy and service risk profile.

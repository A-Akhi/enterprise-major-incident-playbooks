# P1 incident execution

## Purpose

This runbook provides a repeatable execution flow for a Priority 1 incident, from declaration through stabilization and closure. Follow local incident policy and change-approval requirements.

## Sequence

1. Declare the incident and assign command roles.
2. Open the bridge and written source of truth.
3. Confirm scope, impact, and dependencies; separate facts from hypotheses.
4. Assess containment options and use the authorized approval path before changes.
5. Coordinate technical triage and record decisions, owners, and next actions.
6. Communicate updates on the agreed cadence.
7. Verify recovery against service-health and customer-impact indicators.
8. Record closure details and hand off follow-up work to problem management and post-incident review.
